Core Operations & Services

Custom New Pallets

We manufacture custom new pallets built specifically for your unique operational requirements. Using high-quality lumber, our expert team designs pallets that optimize your supply chain, reduce product damage, and improve warehouse efficiency. Perfect for specialized equipment or non-standard load dimensions.

Recycled & Reconditioned Pallets

Our recycled and reconditioned pallets offer an eco-friendly and budget-conscious alternative to new pallets without sacrificing reliability. We meticulously inspect, repair, and grade every recycled pallet to ensure it meets strict performance standards for your supply chain.

Heat Treated (ISPM-15) Pallets

Ensure smooth international shipping with our ISPM-15 certified heat-treated pallets. We strictly adhere to international phytosanitary standards to prevent the spread of wood-borne pests, guaranteeing your goods clear customs without costly delays or quarantines.

Pallet Retrieval & Buy-Back Program

Don't let unused pallets clutter your valuable warehouse space. Our retrieval and buy-back program provides efficient removal of your surplus or broken pallets. We pay competitive rates for reusable pallets and responsibly recycle those at the end of their lifecycle.
